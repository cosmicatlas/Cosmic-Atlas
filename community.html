Community guidelines

  • Label your claims. Say whether you are sharing established evidence, a mainstream theory, a hypothesis, or your own speculation.
  • Unconventional ideas are welcome in Theoretical Ideas. Explain your reasoning and give evidence where you can.
  • Popular does not mean proven. A frequently discussed idea is not a confirmed one.
  • Criticize ideas, not people. Stay respectful. Corrections are welcome.
  • Cite reliable sources such as NASA, ESA, and peer-reviewed papers.
  • Protect privacy. Do not share your or anyone's address, phone number, school, or other personal details.
  • No spam, threats, harassment, or impersonation.

Reporting a problem

On any GitHub discussion or comment, open the "…" menu and choose Report content for spam, harassment, or other abuse. Moderators will review it and may hide, edit, or remove content, or block repeat offenders.

Community posts are written by members and may contain errors. They are not reviewed for scientific accuracy before posting.
