Community guidelines

  • Label observations, established explanations, hypotheses, and personal speculation clearly.
  • Unconventional ideas are welcome, but explain your reasoning and engage constructively with evidence.
  • Criticize ideas, not people. No harassment, threats, impersonation, or spam.
  • Cite reliable sources such as NASA, ESA, or peer-reviewed research.
  • Do not share personal information. Report harmful content to the site moderators.
