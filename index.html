Cosmic Atlas

A universe to explore. A story for you to write.

An astronomy encyclopedia in chapter form, beginning with the Big Bang and moving through the history, structure, and possible future of the universe. This is a work in progress: the chapters are yours to research, draft, revise, and publish one at a time.

Your writing principles

  • Write in your own words and keep notes on sources.
  • Separate observations, established explanations, hypotheses, and unanswered questions.
  • It is fine to publish a short chapter and improve it later.
  • There is no weekly deadline. Publish when you are ready.
