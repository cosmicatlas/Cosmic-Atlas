How claims are labeled

  • Observed evidence
    Something measured directly, such as the temperature of the microwave background.
  • Established theory
    An explanation supported by many independent lines of evidence and widely accepted.
  • Hypothesis
    A proposed explanation that is not yet confirmed. Cosmic inflation is a leading example.
  • Open question
    Something scientists do not yet know.
